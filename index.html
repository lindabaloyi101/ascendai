How we work.

Small, steady steps, each one tested with your team before we move on.

  1. Discover

    We map how work really flows and rank the processes by hours saved.

  2. Design

    We fix the process first. Automating a broken process only breaks it faster.

  3. Build

    We build on your tools, release in small pieces and test with the people who use them.

  4. Hand over

    We train your team to run it, then keep improving month by month.
